# SAFARI FOOTWEAR - Store POS & Daily Ledger

A mobile-friendly daily store management and Point-of-Sale ledger application for SAFARI FOOTWEAR (Main Bazaar Road, Ernakulam, Kochi).

## Features
- Footwear Sales POS: Quick sales entry, auto-calculated discounts, size selector, thermal receipts, and WhatsApp bill sharing.
- Cash & UPI Inflow Tracking: Split incoming payments into Cash and UPI QR collections.
- Supplier Ledger: Track vendor invoices (VKC, Walkaroo, Relaxo, Metro Leather, Paragon), payments, and balances without double-counting.
- Staff Attendance & Shop Hours: 1-tap check-in/out, presence toggles, and monthly payroll advances summary.
- Day-End Reconciliation: Physical currency note counter (₹500 to ₹10) with automated cash variance detection.
- Role Permissions & Privacy: Owner full access with 1-click approvals; Staff mode with sensitive profit and drawings hidden.
- Reports Hub: Daily, weekly, and monthly statements with PDF download and Excel/CSV export.
